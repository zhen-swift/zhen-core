# Part II · The engine that runs everywhere

**(yes, on your iPhone too)**

![The same engine, on a Mac and on an iPhone or iPad, talking to the same network.](figures/p2-hero.svg)

Nothing in this part needs root, and all of it is built for iOS. In this part:

1. **Finding hosts.** One ping socket, every host, no password (chapter 5).
2. **Knocking on ports.** TCP, banners and certificates (chapter 6), then UDP, where silence means "maybe" (chapter 7).
3. **Where am I, and who's there?** Picking the right network (chapter 8) and learning names from Bonjour, NetBIOS and SSDP (chapter 9).
4. **Being smart on top.** Timing, and the Local Network permission (chapter 10).
