# Part III · The Mac-only superpowers

![On a Mac, Zhen can wear the crown and raise the periscope. On an iPhone or iPad, those doors stay locked.](figures/p3-hero.svg)

Parts I and II worked anywhere Darwin runs. This part doesn't. Each chapter here uses something the Mac's kernel offers and the iPhone's kernel keeps locked away from apps.

**In this part**

- **11 · BPF: a periscope on the wire.** Zhen sends and reads raw Ethernet frames, so it can ask every host "who are you?" in ARP.
- **12 · The castle's address book.** Reading the kernel's own ARP table, which needs no root at all.
- **13 · Half-open knocks.** The SYN scan, and guessing a host's operating system from one polite packet.
- **14 · Root, but tiny.** A small privileged helper opens the periscope and hands the app the ticket.

Every chapter ends with **📱 On iPhone & iPad**, and here the answer is mostly ❌. Part IV covers what the iPhone gets instead.
