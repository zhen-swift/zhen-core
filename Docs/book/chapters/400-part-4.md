# Part IV · The iOS lens

![The same engine, in your pocket. Same kernel family, smaller gate.](figures/p4-hero.svg)

Parts I to III took the engine apart on a Mac. This part puts it in your pocket and asks
one question three ways: **what's left when there's no crown?**

In this part:

- **Chapter 15 · What your iPhone gets.** Mac and iPhone, row by row. The one fork in the
  code where the two part ways, and why the iPhone stays quiet about what it can't do.
- **Chapter 16 · From a tap to a syscall.** Follow one tap on **Scan** down twelve steps
  to the first `socket()`, then follow the answers back up two different channels.
- **Chapter 17 · What only a real device can answer.** The simulator runs on the Mac's
  kernel, so some questions are still open. The app's **Diagnostics** screen exists to
  answer them, and you can help.
